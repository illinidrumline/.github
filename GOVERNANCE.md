# Governance

The `illinidrumline` GitHub organization is maintained by a single owner, [@pngdeity](https://github.com/pngdeity), who is responsible for repositories, membership, and access.

Membership is limited to current maintainers of the drumline's public tooling. At present there are no additional members or teams.

Decisions about repositories, branding, and published content rest with the owner. If the drumline designates additional maintainers in the future, this document will be updated to describe roles and review expectations.

This organization is not part of the University of Illinois' production infrastructure and does not speak for the university or the Marching Illini.
